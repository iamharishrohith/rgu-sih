1. **Zero Recall Factor:** SIH Jury members review 50-100 teams per problem statement. Generic names like *Tech Titans*, *Binary Brains*, or *Team Nexus* fail to stand out.
2. **National Database Collisions:** Hundreds of colleges across India submit identical generic names, making identification difficult.
3. **Loss of Technical Context:** A domain-specific name (e.g., *VocalShield* for Voice Cloning) instantly signals the team's solution before they even speak.

## PART 2: The 3-Rule Formula for an Unforgettable Team Name

1. **Formula A: [Domain Core] + [Action / Security Anchor]**
   - Voice Cloning $\rightarrow$ **VocalShield**, **AcoustiGuard**
   - Cyber Threats $\rightarrow$ **TraceSentry**, **SentinEx**
   - Public Transport $\rightarrow$ **UrbanFleet AI**, **TransitIQ**

2. **Formula B: [Indic / Sanskrit Heritage] + [Tech Innovation]** *(Jury Favorite)*
   - Surveillance / CCTV $\rightarrow$ **NetraAI**, **DrishtiLens**
   - National Defense / Cyber $\rightarrow$ **KavachCore**, **RakshaPulse**
   - Land & Urban Property $\rightarrow$ **BhoomiNet**, **KshetraAI**

3. **Formula C: [Product Studio / 1-Word Impact]**
   - Replaces generic group names $\rightarrow$ **Syntrix**, **ZenithLabs**, **Lumina**, **Vektor**
